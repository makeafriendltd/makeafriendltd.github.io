Make a Friend Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 — applies to the Make a Friend personal AI assistant application and its public website at www.makeafriend.net.

1. Who we are (data controller)

This Privacy Policy is issued by Make a Friend Ltd ("Make a Friend", "we", "us"). We build and operate an AI assistant application and we provide AI consulting services. This policy covers our website and the assistant application itself. We are the data controller for the personal data described below. You can reach our data protection contact at privacy@makeafriend.net.

2. What our app does, in plain words

Make a Friend is a personal AI assistant. You ask it to handle tasks — answering questions, drafting and summarizing, researching and comparing options, planning and following work through — and it uses the accounts and services you choose to connect to do that work, showing you results and asking for your approval before anything consequential. Google is one of the services you can connect; it is not the only one. We collect only the minimum personal data each feature needs, we do not sell personal data, and we do not use your mail or file content for advertising.

3. Personal data we collect

The following categories cover the assistant and its optional connections. Visiting our public homepage or this policy does not require a Google account or grant access to your Google data. Google sign-in identifies your account; access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar or Contacts requires separate permissions. Only the connections you enable and permissions you grant apply to you.

Google user data accessed and the user-facing purpose

  • Google account identity: your account identifier, name, email address and profile picture identify your account and display your profile. Google sign-in alone does not let us read your mailbox or files.
  • Gmail, when you enable mail features: message and thread identifiers, sender and recipient addresses, subject lines, timestamps, message bodies and attachments needed for your task. These support finding a message, summarizing a thread, extracting tasks and drafting a reply. Sending or modifying a message requires the relevant granted permission and your approval.
  • Google Drive, when you enable file features: file identifiers, names, types, metadata and content needed to find, summarize or answer questions about a file. Creating or updating a file requires the relevant granted permission and your instruction.
  • Google Calendar, when you enable scheduling features: calendar and event identifiers, titles, descriptions, dates, times, time zones and attendee details needed to plan a task or coordinate a schedule. Changes require the relevant granted permission and your approval.
  • Google Contacts, when you enable contact features: contact names and contact details needed to find the person you ask the assistant to work with. We do not use contacts to create advertising audiences.
  • Connection credentials: OAuth access tokens and, when issued, refresh tokens authorize requests to the connected account. These are not your Google password. They are restricted to the connection you authorized and removed when that connection is disconnected.

The permissions shown on Google's consent screen determine which of these operations are authorized. We do not request unused permissions for possible future features. Additional access requires an additional permission request, not merely a change to this policy.

4. Google user data, AI processing and Limited Use

Make a Friend's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. These restrictions also apply to summaries, embeddings and other data derived from Google user data.

How the assistant processes your Google data

For a task you request, the assistant retrieves the relevant authorized content, combines it with your instructions and processes it with an AI model to produce an answer, summary, draft or plan. If an external AI provider is used, the task instructions and relevant content are sent to that provider for inference — generating your result — not for training a shared model. We store the results and context described in section 7. A task spanning connections may combine relevant information from those connections only to perform the task you requested.

No general-purpose AI training or prohibited uses

  • We do not use Google Workspace API data to develop, improve or train non-personalized AI or machine-learning models. This includes general-purpose or shared models, and data that has been aggregated, anonymized or derived from your Google content.
  • We do not permit third-party AI providers to use Google user data or its derivatives to train or improve their general-purpose models. Providers handling that data must be configured and contractually restricted to the processing needed to deliver your requested feature.
  • We do not sell Google user data or transfer it to advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers. We do not use it for targeted, personalized or retargeted advertising, creditworthiness or lending decisions.
  • Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features described here. Keeping context for your own assistant is not permission to train a model shared with other users.

Human access and permitted transfers

Our staff and contractors are not allowed to read your Google content simply because they can operate the service. Human access is limited to specific data you affirmatively authorize us to view (for example for support), necessary security investigations, legal requirements, or aggregated internal operations permitted by Google's Limited Use requirements. Transfers are limited to delivering your requested user-facing feature with your consent, security purposes, applicable legal obligations, or a business transfer with your explicit prior consent. Our employees, contractors and processors must follow these restrictions.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • Google: account authentication and requests to the Google APIs you connected. An approved action, such as saving a document, sends the result back to the relevant Google service.
  • Other services you connect: we send the instructions and content needed for an action you request in that service. Your Google data is not shared with another connection merely because both are enabled.
  • AI inference providers: where an external model processes a task, it receives the instructions and relevant content needed to produce your result. It must process Google data only for that task, subject to the no-training and Limited Use restrictions above.
  • Hosting, storage and operational providers: these process account data, stored content and technical logs as necessary to host, secure and operate the assistant. They are bound by confidentiality and purpose restrictions.
  • Recipients you instruct us to contact: an email recipient or a connected workspace receives content only when you authorize the relevant sharing action. We do not share your personal assistant content with unrelated consulting customers.
  • Authorities: where disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable law or address a security threat, subject to the Google-data restrictions in section 4.

The data transferred depends on the feature and task you request; we do not transfer an entire mailbox or drive to an AI provider merely because you connected it. Third-party services you deliberately interact with have their own privacy policies. Our processors must use data only for the purposes described here.

6. Where data is stored; transfers

Data is hosted on major cloud providers located in the European Union and the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (or an adequacy decision, where available) and appropriate security measures.

7. Retention

  • Account and settings: kept while your account exists, then deleted within 30 days of account deletion, except information legally required to be retained.
  • Connection credentials: removed when you disconnect the service. Revoking Google's permissions prevents further authorized Google API requests, but does not by itself erase answers or documents already saved in your assistant account.
  • Raw connected-service content: processed for the task, not retained as a full mailbox or drive mirror, and discarded within 30 days. Content you explicitly save in a conversation or memory follows the next rule.
  • Saved conversations, uploads, results and personal memory: retained while your account is active so you can return to your work. A deletion request for an item or your account removes the corresponding stored content within 30 days. This includes stored Google-derived content and associated retrieval data.
  • Approvals and activity records: retained for up to 12 months. Legally required transaction records may be retained longer for that obligation, not for unrelated product use.
  • Server and application logs: kept for a maximum of 12 months.
  • Inquiry emails: kept for a maximum of 36 months, then deleted unless a contract or dispute requires longer.

To delete data without finding an in-app control, email privacy@makeafriend.net from your account email address and identify the connection, conversation, memory or account you want deleted. We verify the request, remove the corresponding stored data and instruct relevant processors to delete copies they hold on our behalf. Any information retained solely for a legal obligation is restricted to that purpose. Disconnecting a service does not delete the source emails, files or events in that service.

8. Security

Google user data, connection credentials and stored assistant content are protected using HTTPS/TLS in transit and encryption at rest for managed storage. Access is limited to authorized systems and personnel through least-privilege permissions, restricted credentials and multi-factor authentication for production access. Operational logs are used to investigate faults and unauthorized access. We do not request your Google password, publish connection tokens or make your private content publicly accessible. No transmission or storage method is perfectly secure.

9. Your rights and controls

You can disconnect a linked service, request deletion of saved content or memory, and request deletion of your account. To revoke Google access independently of the app, open your Google Account's third-party connections, select Make a Friend and remove its access. Revocation stops future authorized access; to also erase stored Google-derived results, use the deletion procedure in section 7 or email privacy@makeafriend.net.

You may request access to your personal data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, withdrawal of consent and portability, and object to processing where applicable. We verify your identity before disclosing or deleting private account data and respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection supervisory authority.

10. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We revise the "Last updated" date and notify users of material changes by email or a prominent in-app notice. Before accessing new categories of Google user data or using that data for a newly disclosed purpose, we update the policy and obtain any additional consent required. A policy update never overrides Google's Limited Use restrictions or silently expands the permissions you granted.
