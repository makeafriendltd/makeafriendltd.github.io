Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 — applies to www.makeafriend.net and the Make a Friend AI assistant application on all platforms.

1. Who we are (data controller)

This Privacy Policy is issued by Make a Friend Ltd ("Make a Friend", "we", "us"). We build and operate an AI assistant application and we provide AI consulting services. This policy covers our website and the assistant application itself. We are the data controller for the personal data described below. You can reach our data protection contact at privacy@makeafriend.net.

2. What our app does, in plain words

Make a Friend is a personal AI assistant. You ask it to handle tasks — answering questions, drafting and summarizing, researching and comparing options, planning and following work through — and it uses the accounts and services you choose to connect to do that work, showing you results and asking for your approval before anything consequential. Google is one of the services you can connect; it is not the only one. We collect only the minimum personal data each feature needs, we do not sell personal data, and we do not use your mail or file content for advertising.

4. How we use Google API data — our commitments

For any data we access through Google APIs (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, etc.) we adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we:

  • access, transfer, and use Google API user data only for the purposes described in section 3, and only while your consent remains in force;
  • do not use Gmail or other Google API user data for advertising, and do not collect it to determine who to show ads to;
  • do not manual-copy, manual-download, or manually extract Gmail content except to provide customer support that you request or when required by law;
  • do not combine Google API user data with data from other sources unless it is needed for a feature you enabled and we disclose here, or unless you consent;
  • restrict access to Google API user data to authorized personnel and processing systems that need it for the feature or for support you requested, and keep access logged.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • Google (authentication and the Google APIs you connect) — authentication requests go through Google's OAuth service under Google's own privacy policy;
  • Other providers whose services you connect — when you link a service, requests to that service are made with the credentials you authorised, under that provider's terms and privacy policy;
  • AI model and infrastructure providers we contract with (for example hosted large-language-model providers), who process submitted content solely to generate results for us under data-processing agreements;
  • Hosting and infrastructure vendors (hosting, database, monitoring, email delivery) acting as processors under contractual obligations;
  • Our business customers — when we build an application for a customer, the usage data of that deployment belongs to the customer and is processed on their instructions;
  • Legal / authorities, when disclosure is required by law or to defend our legal rights.

Recipients process data only on our instructions and for the purposes in this policy.

6. Where data is stored; transfers

Data is hosted on major cloud providers located in the European Union and the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (or an adequacy decision, where available) and appropriate security measures.

7. Retention

  • Account, settings and connection data: kept while your account exists; deleted within 30 days after you delete your account or revoke access.
  • Connected-service content (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, other connectors): processed on demand; we store derived results and the memory described in section 3, not full copies of your mailbox or drive. Raw processed content is discarded within 30 days unless required for a feature you enabled.
  • Conversations, uploads, drafts and memory: kept while your account is active so the assistant keeps context; you can delete individual items or your memory at any time, and it is erased within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Approvals and activity log: kept for 12 months, or longer where needed to evidence a transaction you asked us to perform.
  • Server and application logs: kept for a maximum of 12 months.
  • Inquiry emails: kept for a maximum of 36 months, then deleted unless a contract or dispute requires longer.

8. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS in transit, encryption at rest for managed storage, least-privilege access, unique per-service credentials, audit logging, and regular backups. Access to production data requires multi-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; we work to commercial best standards to protect your data.

9. Your rights and controls

In the app you can see and revoke any connection, review the activity log, and review or delete what the assistant remembers. Independently, you may at any time and free of charge: (a) obtain access to and a copy of your personal data we hold; (b) have inaccurate data corrected; (c) have your data erased; (d) restrict or object to processing; (e) withdraw consent (for example by revoking our OAuth access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all further Google API processing); (f) data portability. Contact privacy@makeafriend.net to exercise these rights; we answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection supervisory authority.

10. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify registered users by email and/or a prominent notice on the application before the change takes effect.
