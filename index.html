Our application

Make a Friend is a general-purpose AI assistant for everyday work and personal admin. Its purpose is to take busywork off your plate: instead of you searching, copying, comparing and chasing things by hand, you give the assistant the task and it does the legwork — then shows you the result for approval. It is not tied to one provider: it works with the accounts, apps and services you choose to connect, and with the AI models we operate and integrate.

What it can do

  • Answer and explain: ask questions in plain language and get answers, definitions, comparisons and step-by-step guidance on demand.
  • Write and draft: emails, replies, notes, summaries, briefs, plans and documents — in your tone, ready for you to review.
  • Read and summarize: long email threads, documents and files become short, accurate summaries with the key decisions and deadlines pulled out.
  • Research and compare: gather information, compare options and produce a recommendation with its reasoning, so you decide faster.
  • Plan and follow through: break a goal into steps, track the moving parts, prepare what is needed and remind you or follow up at the right moment.
  • Work across your connected accounts: with your permission, the assistant can work with the services you link — mail, calendar, files and documents, tasks and other tools — so it can act on the information instead of you relaying it.
  • Keep context: your preferences, ongoing projects and previous decisions are remembered, so you do not repeat yourself; you can review, edit or delete that memory.

You stay in control

  • You decide which accounts and services the assistant may access — nothing is connected by default.
  • Consequential actions (sending a message, changing or deleting something, booking or spending) are held for your explicit approval.
  • An activity log shows what the assistant did, and every connection can be revoked at any time.
  • AI output can be wrong: treat drafts and findings as proposals and check anything important before you rely on it.

How it works with Google

Google is one of the services you can connect. When you do, the assistant connects to your Google Account through Google's permission screen, and it uses only the Google data you explicitly allow — for example reading and summarizing the Gmail messages and Drive files you choose, searching that content to find the right email or document for a task, and saving drafts or documents back to your account when you ask it to. We request only the Google scopes a feature needs, we use that data solely to deliver the feature for the user who granted it, and we do not sell it or use Gmail and Drive content for advertising.
