What we do

End-to-end AI integration for companies that want working intelligent features, not slideware.

LLM integration

Connect ChatGPT-class models to your product: assistants, document Q&A, summarization, classification, and generation pipelines tuned to your domain data.

Google Workspace & OAuth

Secure sign-in and data access for Gmail, Drive, Calendar, and Photos with properly scoped OAuth — including consent-screen, verification, and privacy compliance.

Retrieval & knowledge bases

Vector search and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) so your app answers from your own up-to-date knowledge, not the model's guesses.

Automation & agents

Workflow automation that links your APIs, CRM, and internal tools so routine work runs itself and people review only the exceptions.

Deployment & operations

Hosting, monitoring, prompt/version management, and cost control — we ship to production and keep it healthy.

Team enablement

Hands-on training so your engineers and operators can maintain and extend the AI features after we hand over.
