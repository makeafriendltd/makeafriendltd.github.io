Our application

Makeafriend AI Assistant is a general-purpose personal AI assistant for everyday work and personal admin. Ask for help with a question, a draft, a comparison or a plan, and review its results. You choose the models and accounts it works with. The assistant's conversations, preferences, saved memory, configuration and task files stay in the environment you control rather than a Makeafriend-hosted assistant database.

What it can do

  • Answer and explain: ask questions in plain language and get answers, definitions, comparisons and step-by-step guidance on demand.
  • Write and draft: emails, replies, notes, summaries, briefs, plans and documents — in your tone, ready for you to review.
  • Read and summarize: long email threads, documents and files become short, accurate summaries with the key decisions and deadlines pulled out.
  • Research and compare: gather information, compare options and produce a recommendation with its reasoning, so you decide faster.
  • Plan and follow through: break a goal into steps, track the moving parts, prepare what is needed and remind you or follow up at the right moment.
  • Work across your connected accounts: with your permission, the assistant can work with the services you link — mail, calendar, files and documents, tasks and other tools — so it can act on the information instead of you relaying it.
  • Keep context locally: save preferences, ongoing work and previous decisions in files in your environment; you control how long those files are kept and when to remove them.

You stay in control

  • You decide which accounts and services the assistant may access — nothing is connected by default.
  • Consequential actions (sending a message, changing or deleting something, booking or spending) are held for your explicit approval.
  • You control the local files and any backups. Revoke permissions at the connected service when you no longer want the assistant to access it.
  • AI output can be wrong: treat drafts and findings as proposals and check anything important before you rely on it.

Choose your model

The assistant is model-agnostic: configure a compatible local model or hosted model endpoint rather than being locked to a single provider. A model running entirely in your environment can process its inputs there. A hosted endpoint receives the prompt and task context sent to it, including any relevant connected-service content. That provider's own retention practices and account settings apply; we cannot promise that every external provider stores nothing.

If Google data is included, the endpoint must meet Google's Limited Use and no-generalized-training requirements. Do not route Google data to a provider that uses those inputs to train shared models. Use a protected inference endpoint or a local model instead.

Why the assistant requests Google user data

Google is an optional connection, alongside other services you choose to link. Different permissions serve different features; connecting Google for identity does not automatically grant access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar or Contacts.

  • Account identity: your name, email address, account identifier and profile picture identify the Google account connected in your environment; they do not create a Makeafriend-hosted assistant database.
  • Gmail: authorized message content and metadata let the assistant find an email, summarize a conversation, extract tasks or prepare a reply. Sending or changing mail requires the relevant permission and your approval.
  • Drive: authorized file content and metadata let the assistant find documents, summarize them and answer your questions. Writing a result back requires the relevant permission and your instruction.
  • Calendar and Contacts: when you enable those features, authorized events and contact details help coordinate a schedule or identify the person you ask the assistant to work with. Changes require the relevant permission and your approval.

Only enabled features and the permissions you grant apply. Requested content is processed for your task and any saved assistant data is held in your local files. A selected hosted model may receive relevant content for inference; it must not use Google Workspace data to develop, improve or train non-personalized AI/ML models. We do not sell Google data or use it for advertising. The Privacy Policy explains local storage, external processing, retention, deletion and revocation.

Your data, in plain words

The assistant's saved data belongs in local files in your environment. Make a Friend Ltd does not operate an online store of your assistant conversations, documents, memory or Google connection credentials. External services still receive requests when you choose to use them.

Local files, your control

Conversations, configuration and saved context remain in your environment. You control file permissions, retention and backups; removing the software may not remove its data files.

Your choice of model

Use a local model to keep model processing in your environment, or configure a hosted endpoint. Review that endpoint's privacy and retention terms before sending personal content.

Deletion and access

Delete local files and backup copies you no longer need, and revoke service permissions separately. We cannot remotely erase your local environment. See the Privacy Policy.
