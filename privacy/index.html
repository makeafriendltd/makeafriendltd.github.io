Makeafriend AI Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026. This policy covers Makeafriend AI Assistant and its public website, www.makeafriend.net.

1. Who we are and how the application works

Make a Friend Ltd ("we", "us") develops Makeafriend AI Assistant, a personal AI assistant for research, writing, summarizing, planning and tasks involving accounts you choose to connect. The application is model-agnostic: you choose and configure the AI model or model endpoint rather than being limited to a single provider.

Assistant data is stored as local files in the user's own environment. We do not store your assistant conversations, documents, memory, connection credentials or Google user data in an online database operated by Make a Friend Ltd. You control the environment and its files, access permissions, retention and backups.

Local storage does not mean every operation is offline. Connecting to Google requires communication with Google. Choosing a hosted AI model sends task inputs to that model endpoint, and an action you approve may send content to a connected service or recipient. These transfers are explained below; they are separate from where the assistant saves its own data.

2. Data accessed and the purposes for using it

The application processes the information you supply and the data needed for an enabled connection. Permissions are limited to the features you authorize. Visiting this public website or policy does not give the application access to your Google account.

3. Local storage and user-selected model processing

Local application files

The assistant saves its application data in files in your environment. We do not provide a central online store of that data or automatically keep a separate copy on our servers. If you synchronize those files, include them in backups or run the application in an environment hosted by someone else, those copies are governed by your choices and that environment's arrangements.

A local model

When you configure a model running entirely in your environment, the model can process the task there without sending its inputs to an external AI inference provider. Any Google or other connected-service request still communicates with the service you authorized.

A hosted model or external endpoint

When you choose a hosted model, the configured endpoint receives the prompt and any task context included in the request. That context can contain the Google content the assistant is processing for you. The endpoint processes those inputs to return an answer, summary, draft or plan. The model name, endpoint and configuration you select determine the recipient; there is no single AI provider selected for every user by this policy.

We cannot promise that every user-selected external provider stores nothing. Its own terms, retention practices, location and account settings apply to the requests it receives. Check them before sending personal information. Local application storage is not a guarantee of zero retention by a provider you choose. If you require model inputs to remain in your environment, use a local model and review any other enabled network connections.

4. Google API data and Limited Use

Makeafriend AI Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The restrictions cover raw Google data and summaries, embeddings or other derivatives.

  • Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing assistant features you request and authorize, not for unrelated purposes.
  • We do not use Google Workspace API data to develop, improve or train non-personalized AI or machine-learning models. Generating an answer for your task is inference, not permission to train a general-purpose model.
  • Model-agnostic support does not waive Google's requirements. Google data must not be sent to a model provider whose terms or configuration allow its use for generalized model training or other prohibited uses. Use an endpoint with the necessary no-training protections, or a local model. If those protections cannot be established, do not route Google data to that endpoint.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, provide it to data brokers or information resellers, or use it for creditworthiness or lending decisions.
  • Combining content from connections is limited to the task you requested. Connecting two services is not permission to transfer all of one service's data to the other.

We do not have routine access to your local assistant files. If you choose to send specific Google content to us for support, we need your affirmative permission to view that content. Any human access or transfer must also comply with Google's Limited Use exceptions for security, applicable law and other permitted purposes. Making a local copy does not remove those restrictions.

5. Transfers and recipients

  • Google: OAuth authorization and requests for the data or actions you authorize. Original emails, files and calendar events remain in your Google account under Google's own policies. Results you explicitly save back to Google are stored by that service.
  • Your selected model endpoint: a hosted endpoint receives the task inputs and context sent for inference. A model running entirely locally does not require that external AI transfer. Section 4 applies whenever inputs contain Google data.
  • Connected services and intended recipients: an instruction you approve, such as sending a reply or saving a document, transmits the relevant content to that service or recipient. Your authorization, permissions and the destination service's policies apply.
  • Make a Friend Ltd: we receive the information you voluntarily send us, such as a support email or files you deliberately attach. Do not send passwords, API keys, OAuth tokens or unrelated private content with a support request.

We do not operate an online assistant-data store for these transfers. External processing and storage at a connected service are not the same as Makeafriend retaining your local application files.

6. Retention and deletion

You control retention of the assistant's local files. They remain in your environment until you remove them or your own storage policies remove them. There is no Makeafriend-hosted assistant database with a separate 30-day deletion schedule.

  • To remove saved conversations, documents, memory, configuration or logs, stop the assistant as appropriate and delete the corresponding files in its configured data locations.
  • Remove local connection credentials when disconnecting a service. Revoke Google access independently in your Google Account's third-party connections by selecting Makeafriend AI Assistant and removing its access.
  • Revoking access stops further authorized Google requests; it does not delete files already saved locally. Deleting local files does not delete original emails or documents in Google or copies already sent to another service.
  • Check backups, synchronized folders, copies and any administrator-managed storage separately. Uninstalling software may leave its data files or backups behind.
  • For content sent to a hosted model or connected service, use that provider's deletion mechanisms and account controls. We cannot remotely erase copies held by an independent endpoint you configured.

We cannot remotely inspect or delete your local environment. You can contact privacy@makeafriend.net for guidance. For information you sent directly to us in a support inquiry, you can request access, correction or deletion; we retain that correspondence only as needed to handle the inquiry and applicable legal obligations.

7. Security and user control

The security of local files depends on the environment you control. Restrict file and backup permissions, protect the device or account, and use disk or storage encryption where appropriate. This policy does not claim that every local file is automatically encrypted by the application.

Use HTTPS/TLS endpoints for hosted models and connected services, protect your model keys and OAuth credentials, and do not expose the assistant's data directory publicly. We do not request your Google password. Only grant permissions needed for the task, and review proposed actions and AI output before relying on them. No software, storage or network method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. The public website and contacting us

The public website is separate from the local assistant. Our website hosting and delivery services, GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, process ordinary web-request information such as an IP address, requested URL, timestamp and browser details to deliver and secure these pages. Their operational processing is not storage of your assistant conversations or connected Google content. This website does not require Google authorization to display its homepage or policy.

If you email us, we and our email service receive your address, message and any attachments you send. That correspondence is separate from the local assistant data we do not hold. We do not use the assistant's local content to run advertising.

9. Privacy requests

For data held in your own environment, use your local files and access controls to inspect, export, correct or delete it. For personal information we receive directly, contact privacy@makeafriend.net to request access, correction, deletion or other rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity. Requests concerning a third-party provider's own processing should also be directed to that provider. You may complain to your relevant data-protection authority where applicable.

10. Children

Makeafriend AI Assistant is not directed to children under 16. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us.

11. Changes to this policy

Changes are published on this page with an updated date. Changes to Google-data access or purposes require updated disclosures and the relevant user consent before that new processing occurs. Neither a policy update nor a new model configuration overrides Google's Limited Use restrictions.
